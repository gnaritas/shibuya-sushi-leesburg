Sushi & nigiri

Nigiri and maki, the heart of the program.

Shibuya is a sushi bar first — nigiri and maki rolls lead the menu, plated izakaya-style for the whole table to work through over a long evening.

1Nigiri

Nigiri is the purest line on any sushi menu: a slice of fish laid over a small bed of pressed rice, finished at the counter and handed to the table. It is the first thing reported on Shibuya's menu, and the selection moves with the night.

2Maki rolls

Maki — nori, rice, and fillings rolled tight and cut into pieces — is the sharing half of the sushi program, reported alongside the nigiri at opening.

3How it lands on the table

In an izakaya, nothing arrives all at once and nothing is meant to sit. Order sushi alongside the hot plates — gyoza, shumai, the mini spicy tacos — and let the evening stack itself course by course. See the izakaya plates and the bar program.
