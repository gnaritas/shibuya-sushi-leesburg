Privacy

What this site keeps.

The message form on this site collects your name, phone number, email address, and message. Those details are used for one purpose: to answer you. They go to the restaurant's own message system; nothing is sold, and nothing is shared with advertisers.

No trackers

This site loads no third-party analytics, no advertising pixels, and no tracking cookies. The only third-party embed is the Google Maps window on the home page, which Google handles under its own privacy policy.
