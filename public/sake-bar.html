Sake & the bar

A sake-first bar, named for the district that never eats early.

Shibuya takes its name from the Tokyo district famous for its dining and nightlife — narrow izakaya stacked shoulder to shoulder, the city eating and drinking late. The bar program here leads with sake, the way the name promises.

1Sake, first

Sake is named on the front of the program — the natural partner for nigiri and maki, and the drink an izakaya evening is built around.

2The full bar behind it

The room's licensing notice filed with the Virginia ABC authority covers restaurant service with wine, beer, and mixed beverages, on and off premises. Sake leads; the bar behind it is the full kind.

3Ordering with the room in mind

The izakaya rhythm is drink-and-plate all night: a carafe of sake, a plate of gyoza, then the next round before the first one is finished. See the small plates and the sushi program.
