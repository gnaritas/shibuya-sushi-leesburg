The izakaya way

Hot small plates, built for sharing.

An izakaya is a Japanese neighborhood eatery built around exactly this: a table of small hot and cold plates, ordered as the evening goes, beside drinks that keep pace. Shibuya brings that format to the King Street corner.

1Gyoza

Pan-fried dumplings — the workhorse plate of every izakaya kitchen, crisp on one side and on the table minutes after you order.

2Shumai

Steamed dumplings, the softer counterpart to the gyoza, reported on Shibuya's opening menu.

3Edamame

Soybeans in the pod, salted — how the table starts while the rest of the order is still being decided.

4Mini spicy tacos

Reported on the opening menu — a small-plate idea that reads like downtown Leesburg talking back to Tokyo, and the kind of dish an izakaya menu earns its personality with.

5And the ramen

The kitchen works across sushi, ramen, and small plates. The full hot list is growing through soft opening — ask for it when you call (571) 918-0306.
