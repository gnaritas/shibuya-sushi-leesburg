The room

An 1850 building with a habit of feeding Leesburg.

The corner of South King and Loudoun Street SE has served this town for more than 170 years. The room has recent restaurant history of its own — it was home to Ebisu Sushi & Thai, and before that Delirium Cafe. Shibuya — named for the Tokyo district famous for its dining and nightlife — takes that corner up again as a Japanese izakaya.

Good to know

When are you open?

We are in soft opening and currently serving Thursday through Sunday, evening into late night. Opening hours can shift while a new kitchen finds its rhythm — call (571) 918-0306 before heading over.

What is on the menu?

Sushi and nigiri, maki rolls, mini spicy tacos, gyoza, shumai, and edamame, plus more as the menu grows through soft opening. The kitchen works across sushi, ramen, and izakaya small plates.

Do you take reservations?

The quickest line to the room is the phone: (571) 918-0306.

Where exactly are you?

At 101 South King Street in downtown Leesburg — the historic 1850 building on the corner of South King and Loudoun Street SE, across from Shutters on King.
